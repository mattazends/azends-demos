City water or well water

Different water needs a different fix.

On city water, it's safe. It just isn't great.

Municipal water is treated to be safe, and that treatment is part of the problem: chlorine you can taste, plus hardness minerals that build up in pipes and appliances.

Chlorine taste and smellAdded during treatment
Hardness and scaleTaps, kettles, water heaters
Dry skin and hairCommon with hard water
Old pipesWhat happens after the street

Usually: whole-home filter and softener, with RO at the kitchen tap.

On a private well, nobody else is testing it.

Your town tests city water. Your well is on you. That's why every well home starts with a test kit, and the system is built around the results.

BacteriaTreated with UV, no chemicals
Iron and manganeseOrange and black staining
Sulphur smellThe rotten-egg smell
Very hard waterHeavy scale and spotting

Usually: test first, then UV, iron filter and softener.
