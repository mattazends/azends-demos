04 · What's holding the website back

Ten fixes, biggest impact first.

1

No page for Aylesbury, or any town

The site only says "Buckinghamshire". Google has nothing to rank for Aylesbury, Wendover, Tring or Thame, so it ranks the sites that do.

Fix: a proper page per town with real jobs, photos and reviews from that town, starting with Aylesbury, Wendover, Tring, Thame and Princes Risborough.
Critical
2

Service pages don't mention where you work

Titles like "Expert Tree Pruning Services" and "Efficient Stump Grinding Solutions" have no location. They compete with every tree surgeon in the country.

Fix: e.g. "Stump Grinding Aylesbury | Bucks Tree Surgeons" with local copy and FAQs on each.
Critical
3

Your homepage headline is "Make an Appointment..."

The H1, the line Google reads first, is the form title. There's no meta description and the page title is just "Bucks Tree Surgeons".

Fix: "Tree Surgeon in Aylesbury & Buckinghamshire" as the H1, with a title and description that sell the click.
Critical
4

The site undersells you

It says 149 reviews, Google shows 216. It says nothing about NPTC qualifications or insurance, which your Instagram does. It says "over 10 years", Checkatrade says "over 5".

Fix: live review count, qualifications and insurance on every page, one consistent story everywhere.
High
5

No address on the website

Google Maps has you on Burcott Lane, Bierton. The website has no address at all, so Google can't connect the two.

Fix: name, address and phone in the footer of every page, matching Google exactly.
High
6

Google can't read your reviews or your business

Structured data is just "WebSite" and "Organization". No LocalBusiness, no service area, no review rating, so you miss the gold stars in normal results.

Fix: LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ and review schema.
Medium
7

Dead links in the footer

Blog, Safety Guidelines and Tree Care Tips all go nowhere. Google sees a half-finished site.

Fix: build them as real guides or remove them.
Medium
8

Slow on phones

The homepage loads about 4MB, with one photo at 1.7MB. Most people searching for a tree surgeon are on their phone.

Fix: compressed WebP photos under 200KB, lazy loading.
Medium
9

Zoom is switched off on mobile

The site blocks pinch to zoom, which hurts accessibility and Google's mobile checks. 8 images have no alt text.

Fix: allow zoom, describe every photo with the job and town.
Medium
10

No answers to the questions people ask

Google shows "How much does a tree surgeon cost?" and "How do I check a tree surgeon is qualified?" for your name. You have no page answering them.

Fix: 2 short guides a month: costs, TPOs in Buckinghamshire, best time to prune.
Medium

How we get the website to number one.
