How we work

Every joint done properly. Every time.

Most failed sealant isn't a bad product. It's a rushed joint. The same five steps go into a single shower or a stadium.

1

Survey & price

We price from photos or drawings, or visit site to measure up.

2

Cut out

Old and failed sealant fully removed. Never sealed over.

3

Prep & prime

Joint cleaned, primed and backer rod sized to the gap.

4

Apply & match

Approved sealant, colour matched to the surface around it.

5

Tool & sign off

Tooled to a clean finish, inspected and signed off.
