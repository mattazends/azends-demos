04 · What's holding you back

Ten fixes, biggest impact first.

Most of these are quick. Together they're why a 40-year company is losing to firms that started last year.

1

Your 46 Essex town pages are mostly invisible

The pages exist, but they were moved recently to a 3-level-deep URL (/domestic-mastic-services/mastic-man-essex/mastic-man-chelmsford/) and nothing in your menu links to them. Google has only picked up around 10, and still under the old addresses.

Fix: one Essex hub page linked from the main menu, short URLs like /essex/chelmsford/, every town linked from the hub and from its neighbours, then resubmit in Search Console.
Critical
2

The town pages are 70 to 80% the same copy

The Chelmsford page has around 400 words and only a third mentions Chelmsford. Its photos are labelled "Basildon Mastic Man" and captioned "Wembley Stadium". Google treats near-copies as one page and ranks none of them.

Fix: rewrite the top 15 towns with real local detail: jobs done there, areas and postcodes covered, a local project photo, a local review, a town FAQ.
Critical
3

Google Business Profile name breaks Google's rules

Your listing is called "Fastglobe Mastics - Mastic Man Essex and London". Google's guidelines say extra keywords in the business name aren't allowed and can get the profile suspended. There's also a second listing in Aldridge set as "Manufacturer" with 0 reviews.

Fix: rename to "Fastglobe Mastics", set the main category to a sealant/waterproofing contractor type, add every service, photos weekly, posts weekly. Tidy the Aldridge listing.
Critical
4

15 reviews vs 184

The map pack is where most local calls come from, and review count is a big part of who gets in it. You're 169 behind GT Sealants.

Fix: automatic review request by text after every finished job, with a direct link. Even 15 a month closes the gap within a year.
Critical
5

No phone number anywhere on the website

There's no clickable number on the homepage, Essex pages or domestic page. Only a form and a "WhatsApp us" button with no number. Google and customers both read a visible local number as a trust signal.

Fix: 01708 523921 in the header, hero and footer of every page, as a tap-to-call link.
High
6

Name, address and number don't match anywhere

Every footer shows Canvey Island. Google Maps says 1 Barlow Way. Your contact page says Unit 1H, 9 Barlow Way. Emails are hello@ on the site and info@ on directories. The Durham office uses a Leeds 0113 number.

Fix: pick one exact Rainham head office format and use it on the site, Google, Yell, Companies House listings and directories.
High
7

Homepage is 14.5MB and loads 60 files

Single photos are 1.5 to 2.1MB PNGs. On a phone over 4G that's slow, and page speed feeds into rankings and into how many people wait for the page.

Fix: WebP images under 200KB each, lazy loading, lighter hero. Target under 2MB.
High
8

No meta descriptions, weak titles

The homepage title is "Home - Fastglobe Mastics" and core pages have no meta description at all, so Google writes its own snippet.

Fix: e.g. "Mastic & Sealant Contractors Essex | Since 1985 | Fastglobe" with a description that sells the click.
Medium
9

Google can't read who you are

The only structured data is a generic "WebPage". No LocalBusiness, address, opening hours, service areas or review rating. 28 of 29 homepage images have no alt text, and the "Company founded / Completed projects" counters show no numbers to Google.

Fix: LocalBusiness + Service + FAQ schema, descriptive alt text, real numbers written in the HTML.
Medium
10

No commercial pages for Essex, no guides

Waterproofing, anti-graffiti, movement joints, concrete repair and rope access only have national pages. No blog or cost guides, so nothing ranks for questions like "how much does mastic cost".

Fix: an Essex page per commercial service, plus 2 guides a month answering real questions (cost, how long sealant lasts, mastic vs silicone).
Medium
