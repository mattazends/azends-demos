Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Something else on your mind? Call the studio on 01277 402469.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one working day, from a real person at Fenlux. Never an automated reply.

When will I get a price?

After your consultation we send a written proposal within seven working days. Your price is then fixed against the specification you sign off.

Do you supply and fit?

Yes. Our own team fits every home project. Builders and contractors can choose supply only, or supply and fit.

Which areas do you cover?

Essex, London and Hertfordshire, from the City to the Essex coast.

Can you work on period or listed homes?

Yes. We offer heritage-style windows and have Grade II experience with the City of London Corporation. We talk through planning and conservation needs at your consultation.

What happens after installation?

Your warranty details are kept in one place, and we come back twelve months after handover for a free check-in and service.
