Why do this before the first storm?

Contractors assign crews and routes ahead of the season. A site that signs up mid-winter joins the end of a list. A plan agreed in the fall means your site is mapped, prioritized and pre-treated before the first forecast.

When should we start winter planning?
In the fall, before the first forecast storm. Site walks and contracts take time to set up.
What's the most common gap?
Contracts that cover snow but not ice. Make sure de-icing and pre-treatment are named in the scope.
