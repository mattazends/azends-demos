Who has to clear the sidewalk?

The person in charge of a building or lot with a paved sidewalk must clear it. For commercial property, that means owners, lessees, tenants, occupants or whoever manages the building. A contract with a snow company doesn't move the deadline, so it pays to know your contractor will hit it.

How long do you have?

The deadline depends on when the snow stops falling, not when it starts.

7:00 AM and 4:59 PMWithin 4 hours
5:00 PM and 8:59 PMWithin 14 hours

What counts as 'clear'?

  • A path at least 4 feet wide along the sidewalk
  • Corner properties: clear through to the crosswalk and the pedestrian ramp (curb cut)
  • Snow and ice removed from around fire hydrants
  • Bus stops without a shelter are the property owner's to clear
  • Ice has to be removed too, or treated with salt, sand or similar if it can't be

Where can the snow go?

Not into the street. Shoveling, blowing or pushing snow into the roadway can earn a separate summons. Stack it on your own property or have it hauled away.

What are the fines?

DSNY sets the fine at $100 for a first offense, $150 for a second and $250 for a third. For a business with several frontages, a run of storms adds up quickly, and a fine is the smallest cost of an icy sidewalk.

How to stay ahead of the clock

The easiest deadline to meet is the one you start before the snow does. Pre-treating sidewalks ahead of the storm stops snow bonding to the pavement, so crews working during the storm can keep the path open instead of racing the clock after it stops.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Anything else, call the team on (917) 970-2960. Someone picks up 24/7.

What happens if snow stops at 4 PM?
You have 4 hours, so the sidewalk must be clear by 8 PM.
Does hiring a contractor protect me from fines?
No. The deadline applies to the person in charge of the property. That's why it matters that your contractor works during the storm.
Do I have to clear ice as well as snow?
Yes. Ice that can't be removed has to be covered with salt, sand or a similar material.
