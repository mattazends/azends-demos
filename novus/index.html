FAQ

Questions facility managers ask us.

Anything else, call the team on (917) 970-2960. Someone picks up 24/7.

Do you clear residential properties?
Not private homes or driveways. Novus is commercial only, so business sites never wait behind one. Apartment, co-op and condo buildings are run as commercial properties, and we do cover those. For a private residence, our residential team can help at residences@novusmaintenance.com.
Do you treat before the storm?
Yes. Surfaces are pre-treated before the first flake so snow and ice can't bond to the pavement, then cleared during the storm and re-treated after it. How pre-storm anti-icing works →
Which areas do you cover?
All five boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island, with teams stationed locally and a warehouse in Long Island City. Have sites further out? Tell us and we'll talk it through.
Can you handle multiple locations under one contract?
Yes. We look after businesses with several sites, including clients with locations nationwide. One point of contact, one plan, every site covered.
How fast do crews arrive in a storm?
Crews are on standby 24/7 through the season and stationed across the boroughs, so they're close to your site before the storm starts. Your plan sets out exactly what gets done and in what order.
What does the NYC sidewalk law require?
If snow stops between 7 AM and 4:59 PM you have 4 hours. Between 5 PM and 8:59 PM you have 14 hours. Overnight, between 9 PM and 6:59 AM, it must be clear by 11 AM. DSNY fines are $100 for a first offense, $150 for a second and $250 for a third.
What de-icing products do you use?
Premium, less corrosive de-icing materials that limit ice build-up and stop refreezing, while being kinder to your paving, entrances and flooring.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Novus Maintenance Corp is fully licensed, registered and insured, and associated with the Snow & Ice Management Association (SIMA).
