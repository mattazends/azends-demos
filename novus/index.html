Can you handle multiple locations under one contract?

How fast do crews arrive in a storm?
Crews are on standby 24/7 through the season and stationed across the boroughs, so they're close to your site before the storm starts. Your plan sets out exactly what gets done and in what order.
What does the NYC sidewalk law require?
If snow stops between 7 AM and 4:59 PM you have 4 hours. Between 5 PM and 8:59 PM you have 14 hours. Overnight, between 9 PM and 6:59 AM, it must be clear by 11 AM. DSNY fines are $100 for a first offense, $150 for a second and $250 for a third.
What de-icing products do you use?
Premium, less corrosive de-icing materials that limit ice build-up and stop refreezing, while being kinder to your paving, entrances and flooring.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Novus Maintenance Corp is fully licensed, registered and insured, and associated with the Snow & Ice Management Association (SIMA).
