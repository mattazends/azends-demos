FAQ

Plowing questions.

Anything else, call the team on (917) 970-2960. Someone picks up 24/7.

Do you plow during the storm or after it?
During. Crews work the storm at the depths set in your plan, then finish with a clean-up pass once it stops. Waiting until the end lets snow pack down and freeze.
Where does the snow go?
Into stacking areas agreed in your plan, away from drains, hydrants and sightlines. If your site has no room, we haul it off site.
Can you plow several lots under one contract?
Yes. Multi-site portfolios get one plan, one point of contact and crews staged in each borough.
How do you avoid damaging curbs and islands?
We walk your site before the season and note curbs, islands, drains and anything buried under snow, so crews know the ground in the dark.
