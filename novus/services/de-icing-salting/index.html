The bucket-of-rock-salt problem

More salt isn't more safety.

Salt thrown onto packed ice works slowly, so the surface stays slick through the morning rush.

What's the difference between de-icing and anti-icing?
Anti-icing goes down before the storm to stop ice bonding. De-icing melts ice that has already formed. We use both, and lean on anti-icing because it does more with less.
Will it damage my entrance or flooring?
We use premium, less corrosive materials and apply measured amounts, which means less residue tracked inside and less wear on paving.
How often do you re-treat?
As conditions need it: through the storm and again before overnight temperatures drop, which is when refreeze catches people out.
Do you de-ice without snow, for freezing rain or frost?
Yes. Freezing rain and frost are some of the most dangerous conditions because surfaces look wet, not icy. Your plan covers them.
