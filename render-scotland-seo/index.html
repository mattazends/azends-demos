04 · What's holding you back

Ten fixes, biggest impact first.

1

Three sites splitting one business

Two of them are near copies, so Google ranks one and ignores the other. Every link, review and mention is split three ways instead of building one strong site.

Fix: one main site on renderscotland.com, 301 redirect the other two page by page.
Critical
2

No page for Edinburgh, Livingston or any town

The site lists Edinburgh, Livingston, Glasgow, Aberdeen, Kelso, Dundee and Perth but has no page for any of them. Google needs a page per place to rank you there.

Fix: a proper page for each town with local jobs, photos and reviews, starting with Edinburgh, Livingston, Broxburn and West Lothian.
Critical
3

6 reviews vs 80

Every review is 5 stars, and they mention Andrea, David, Paul and Chris by name. That's great. But Google ranks the map pack heavily on volume, so you show last.

Fix: automatic review request by text the day each job finishes. 10 a month takes you past 80 within the year.
Critical
4

Google Business Profile name is keyword stuffed

It's listed as "Render Scotland - Exterior Rendering Services & Roughcasting Edinburgh". Google's rules say extra keywords in the name aren't allowed and can get the profile suspended. Category is "Plasterer", not rendering.

Fix: rename to "Render Scotland", add rendering and insulation categories and services, post weekly with job photos.
Critical
5

Homepage tells Google nothing

Title "Home - Render Scotland", H1 just "Render Scotland". No mention of Edinburgh, rendering or K-Rend where Google looks first.

Fix: e.g. "Rendering Edinburgh & West Lothian | K-Rend & Insulated Render | Render Scotland".
High
6

Your phone number is wrong online

Facebook shows the mobile as "0770713722", one digit short. The site has no street address but Google Maps shows Thistle Industrial Estate, Broxburn. The site says "Boxburn".

Fix: one exact name, address and number on the site, Google, Facebook, Yell and directories.
High
7

Broken and duplicate links

The "Render Systems" link points to http://Render-Systems, which goes nowhere. Service links mix capital and lowercase URLs, which makes duplicate pages. Blog posts sit under /uncategorized/.

Fix: clean lowercase URLs, a proper /guides/ section, fix the menu.
Medium
8

No proof on the page

There's a "Safety accreditation's" heading with nothing under it. You're SSIP and Acclaim accredited and use Sto, Parex, Dryvit, Knauf and Enewall systems, but none of that is spelled out as text Google can read.

Fix: accreditations, systems, guarantees and years trading written out on every service page.
Medium
9

Google can't read your images or your business

Most images have empty alt text. There's no LocalBusiness schema telling Google your address, area and reviews. The footer still says 2023.

Fix: LocalBusiness, Service and FAQ schema, alt text that describes each job and town.
Medium
10

No answers to the questions people search

"How much does it cost to re-render a house", "does render devalue a house", "how long does insulated render last" all show up in Google's People Also Ask for your name. You have no page answering them.

Fix: 2 short guides a month, each linking to the matching service page.
Medium
