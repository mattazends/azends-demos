Insulated render

A new look outside. A warmer house inside.

Insulated render wraps your walls in insulation, then finishes them in render. Here's what goes on, layer by layer.

  • 01
    AdhesiveBonds the system to your existing wall
  • 02
    Insulation boardEPS or mineral wool, sized to your wall
  • 03
    Mesh & basecoatReinforces the surface against cracking
  • 04
    PrimerGives the topcoat a perfect key
  • 05
    Render topcoatSilicone, K-Rend or textured, in your colour
