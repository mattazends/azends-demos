  1. Screened before we walk in

    Everyone who enters the home has NDIS Worker Screening clearance.

  2. Entry booked around the routine

    We arrange entry with the house supervisor and work around the participant's day, not ours.

  3. We pause when the person is unsettled

    If the participant is unsettled, work stops. We come back when it suits.

  4. Fixed in a way that suits them

    Not just patched. We fix it so the same thing is less likely to happen again.

  5. Photographed and logged

    Before and after photos, what we did and who did it, on one job record.

  6. A record you can hand to an auditor

    Every job and every check, kept the way an auditor asks for it.
