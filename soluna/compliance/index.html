Our approach

Compliance Beyond the Checklist

We combine property safety expertise with an understanding of SIL environments, supporting providers with inspections, coordinated servicing and documentation relevant to their compliance obligations.

  1. Understanding SIL Environments

    Property safety considerations informed by disability support needs and relevant NDIS Practice Standards.

  2. Qualified Safety Services

    Fire, electrical and specialist safety services coordinated with appropriately qualified personnel.

  3. Planned Compliance Schedules

    Coordinated inspections and servicing to support ongoing property safety requirements.

  4. Identifying Safety Risks

    Property reviews to identify environmental hazards, damaged equipment and potential safety concerns.

  5. Clear Compliance Documentation

    Inspection reports, equipment registers and supporting records for provider audits.

  6. Multi-Property Coordination

    Consistent compliance scheduling and documentation across multiple SIL homes.
