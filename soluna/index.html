We provide property maintenance, modifications, renovations and compliance services for disability and aged care environments. Our specialist expertise includes SIL home set-ups, aged care renovations and Robust Plus upgrades.

Yes. We provide complete home set-ups for standard and complex SIL, including furniture, appliances, household essentials, installation and standardised house documentation to support consistent operations and audit preparation.

Yes. From individual room and bathroom upgrades to complete facility renovations, we deliver projects in collaboration with licensed builders and specialist trade partners.

SDA Robust is an official housing design category. Robust Plus is Soluna's specialist upgrade service, adding tailored reinforcement and protective features to existing homes while preserving their comfort and residential character.

Yes, where safe and practical. We coordinate work around residents' routines and support needs, aiming to minimise disruption. Larger renovations may require additional arrangements.
