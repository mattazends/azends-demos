Our story

We started inside supported homes. That's still where we work.

Soluna grew out of facilities work in homes where people with complex needs live. Providers who saw how our team worked kept asking us to look after their houses too. So we made it its own company.

Most trades understand buildings. Fewer understand the people living in them. We built Soluna to do both.

The person comes first

We plan the job around the person's routine, and we stop when they need us to.

Fix it properly

We don't just patch the damage. We fix it so it's less likely to come back.

Show the work

Every job is photographed and logged, so you always know what happened in the house.

Who we work with

Pick the one that sounds like you. We'll talk your language.

The whole site changes its wording to match. A resident in aged care, a participant in SIL, your family member at home.

How a job runs

The difference is how the job runs, not the tools.

Every step is on the job record, so you can see exactly what happened in the house.

  1. Screened before we walk in

    Everyone who enters the home has NDIS Worker Screening clearance.

  2. Entry booked around the routine

    We arrange entry with the house supervisor and work around the participant's day, not ours.

  3. We pause when the person is unsettled

    If the participant is unsettled, work stops. We come back when it suits.

  4. Fixed in a way that suits them

    Not just patched. We fix it so the same thing is less likely to happen again.

  5. Photographed and logged

    Before and after photos, what we did and who did it, on one job record.

  6. A record you can hand to an auditor

    Every job and every check, kept the way an auditor asks for it.
