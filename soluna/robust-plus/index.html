Robust Plus

Built for the hard days. Still looks like a home.

Robust Plus is an upgrade our team does to an existing house, usually one built to the SDA Robust standard. We do it room by room, matched to the person who lives there.

Two tiers

Start with what keeps failing. Go further where it's needed.

Tier 1

The essentials

Stops the repairs that keep coming back.

  • Bedroom: impact-rated wall lining
  • Bedroom: laminated window glazing
  • Bathroom: concealed cistern
  • Bathroom: robust tapware and basin
  • Kitchen: lockable appliance covers
  • Kitchen: cooktop isolation switch
  • Living room: tv behind a clear cover
  • Living room: impact-rated wall lining
Tier 1 + 2

For higher needs

Everything in Tier 1, plus fixed furniture and stronger finishes.

  • Bedroom: bed base fixed down
  • Bedroom: built-in wardrobe
  • Bathroom: slip-resistant floor
  • Bathroom: reinforced grab rails
  • Kitchen: reinforced cabinet doors
  • Kitchen: rounded benchtop edges
  • Living room: heavy furniture fixed down
How a job runs

The difference is how the job runs, not the tools.

Every step is on the job record, so you can see exactly what happened in the house.

  1. Screened before we walk in

    Everyone who enters the home has NDIS Worker Screening clearance.

  2. Entry booked around the routine

    We arrange entry with the house supervisor and work around the participant's day, not ours.

  3. We pause when the person is unsettled

    If the participant is unsettled, work stops. We come back when it suits.

  4. Fixed in a way that suits them

    Not just patched. We fix it so the same thing is less likely to happen again.

  5. Photographed and logged

    Before and after photos, what we did and who did it, on one job record.

  6. A record you can hand to an auditor

    Every job and every check, kept the way an auditor asks for it.
