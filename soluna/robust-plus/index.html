Our approach

Protection Designed Around Individual Needs

Robust Plus combines specialist property reinforcement with an understanding of individual support needs, creating stronger living environments without compromising comfort.

  1. Individual Risk Considerations

    Upgrades informed by the person's support needs, behaviour support plan and relevant professional recommendations.

  2. Room-by-Room Assessment

    Identifying vulnerable areas and determining the appropriate level of protection for each room.

  3. Tailored Reinforcement

    Protective modifications selected for the property's layout, existing features and specific requirements.

  4. Residential Design & Comfort

    Reinforced fixtures and finishes designed to preserve the appearance and comfort of home.

  5. Durable Materials & Installation

    Practical materials, secure installations and protective solutions suited to the intended environment.

  6. Coordinated Delivery & Documentation

    Work coordinated with providers and relevant professionals, with installation details and completion records.
