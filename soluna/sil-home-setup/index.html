A New SIL Home. Without the Set‑up Burden.

Opening a new SIL home involves more than finding a suitable property. Furniture needs purchasing, appliances installing, kitchens equipping, household supplies organising and essential property documentation preparing.

For SIL providers, this often becomes an additional responsibility for management teams and support staff.

Soluna offers a complete home set-up service, bringing these requirements together under one coordinated solution.

Whether you're opening your first SIL home or expanding an existing portfolio, we prepare the property so your team can focus on delivering support.

Standard and complex SIL

Different Support Needs. Different Home Requirements.

Not every SIL home needs the same set-up. Soluna prepares homes for both standard and complex support environments, balancing consistent household standards with individual requirements.

Standard SIL Home Set-ups

Practical, comfortable and functional living environments equipped with the furniture, appliances, household essentials and documentation required for everyday supported living.

Typical considerations
  • Comfortable bedrooms and shared living spaces
  • Fully equipped kitchens and laundry facilities
  • Everyday household equipment and supplies
  • Property safety and operational documentation

Complex SIL Home Set-ups

Homes requiring additional environmental considerations, specialist fixtures, protective modifications or tailored arrangements to accommodate more complex disability support needs.

Additional considerations may include
  • Reinforced walls and protective fixtures
  • Secured furniture and equipment
  • Sensory rooms and calming environments
  • Accessibility and environmental safety modifications

Where additional modifications are required, Soluna can coordinate these through its specialist Modifications and Robust Plus services, informed by relevant professional recommendations.

Every home is assessed according to its layout, intended use and the individual requirements of the people who will live there.

House files & documentation

A Home Set‑up That Goes Beyond Furniture

A well-prepared SIL home also needs organised property information that staff and managers can access when required.

Soluna can establish standardised house documentation based on the provider's approved templates and operational requirements.

These documents support the provider's property management and audit preparation. Participant support plans, clinical records and broader NDIS compliance responsibilities remain with the relevant provider and professionals.

From the physical home to its property records, our goal is to deliver a consistent and organised environment ready for the provider's operational handover.
