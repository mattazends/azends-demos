01 · Launch plan

Wake them up. Then launch.

Most people jump straight to selling and get two sales. We do it in two steps: reactivate your audience, then invite them to a live launch call with a founders offer.

The maths

1,100+people across WhatsApp, members and Instagram
300come back to life after a week of value
50turn up to the launch call
10 to 20join at £500 = £5k to £10k

Even 20 people out of 1,000 is a 2% conversion. That's very doable from a warm audience that already knows you.

One hour. One offer.

A Google Meet with your community. Mostly value and your story, then the founders offer at the end. Keep them talking in the chat the whole way through.

Making it land

Stack the value before the price

Say what each part would cost on its own. A website alone costs people around £2,500. By the time you say £500, it should feel like a steal.

Keep it moving

Ask a question every few minutes. Read out comments. If it goes quiet, bring a student in. Having Matt on as a co-host is an option to keep it flowing (terms agreed before).

Real scarcity only

20 spots and a real deadline. If you say it closes Sunday, it closes Sunday.

04 · Sales calls

Ask, listen, then pitch.

They've already answered 4 questions to book, so you know the basics. Read their answers before every call. Your job is to find out what they really want, then show them how The Source gets them there.

The call 30 minutes

2 min
Open"Thanks for applying. I'll ask you a few questions, then if it's a fit I'll show you how it works. Sound good?"
5 min
Where are you now?What are you doing at the moment? Have you sold anything yet? What's worked, what hasn't?
5 min
Where do you want to be?In 3 months, what would make this worth it? Why now? Why does that matter to you? Keep asking "why" until you hear the real reason.
3 min
What's stopping you?What's got in the way so far? Suppliers, money, time, confidence?
3 min
Can they commit?How many hours a week can you give this? What can you put in, for the mentorship and for stock?
8 min
Show themThree slides: your story and results, your students, and what's in The Source. Tie each part back to what they told you.
4 min
Close"Based on what you've told me, I think this is a fit. Do you want to pay in full or in 3 payments?" Then stop talking.

Objections

"It's too expensive."

"I get it. That's why you can split it into 3 payments of £333. And if you haven't made your money back in 3 months, I keep working with you for free until you do."

"I need to think about it."

"Of course. What specifically do you need to think about?" There's usually one real reason. Find it and answer it.

"I don't have time."

"I built this around college full time and training. How many hours could you give it a week?"

"I need to ask my parents."

"Makes sense. Want to book 15 minutes with them on as well? I'll answer anything they want to ask."

If it's not a fit

Be honest: "I don't think The Source is right for you yet, because of where you are right now. You'd be better off starting with The Plug." That honesty builds trust, and a lot of them upgrade later.

After every call

  1. Fathom records the call and writes it up for you.
  2. Copy the transcript into that student's own Claude project.
  3. Ask Claude for their to-do list from the call, and send it to them.
  4. Paste their WhatsApp messages into the same project as you go, so you never lose track of anyone.

08 · Setup

Get it connected.

Everything that needs your login. Do these this week so the launch isn't held up.

  1. Buy The Source domain (e.g. thesource.store). Check what's free first.
  2. Sign up to Google Workspace with that domain and make your email, e.g. vin@thesource.store. It's a few pounds a month.
  3. Google gives you a TXT record. Paste it into the domain's DNS settings (GoDaddy if you buy it there) to verify it.
  4. Turn on 2-step verification straight away.

Calendly

  1. Sign in to Calendly with the new Workspace account.
  2. One event: "Call with Vin", 30 mins, Google Meet, your 2 evenings a week.
