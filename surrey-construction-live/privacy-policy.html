Privacy policy

Surrey Construction Ltd, 1 Epsom Road, Guildford, Surrey, GU1 3JT. Contact: surrey-constructionltd@outlook.com or 07388 498113.

What we collect

When you ask for a quote we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, and, if you give them, your email, postcode, project details and any notes.

Why we use it

Only to reply about your project and prepare a quote. We rely on your request for a quote (taking steps before a contract) as our lawful basis. We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing.

How it reaches us

Form submissions are sent to our office email inbox by a form delivery service. We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your project, and no longer than 2 years if no work goes ahead.

Cookies

With your permission we use Google Ads cookies to measure which adverts lead to enquiries. If you reject cookies, these are not set. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" at the bottom of our homepage.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you by contacting us using the details above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
