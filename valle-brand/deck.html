01 · The idea

The V becomes the R.
One stroke, shared.

V for Valle. R for Roofing. The right arm of the V is the stem of the R, so the two letters are built from one shape. Valle also means valley, which is exactly where Tucson sits. The mark is a letter, a valley and a roofline at the same time.

02 · Primary mark · Construction
  • 100-unit grid. Cap height 80 (y10 to y90). Everything sits on a 2-unit grid so it redraws cleanly at any size.
  • One stroke weight: 16 units. Stem, arm, bowl and leg are all the same thickness. That is what makes it feel engineered rather than drawn.
  • V arm slope 2:1 (40 across, 80 down). The arm lands on the stem's foot so the V has a hard flat base, like a footing.
  • Bowl is a true half circle, radius 22, centred at (74, 32). Counter radius 6.
  • Leg kicks out at 1:2 and shares the baseline with the V foot. The gap between stem and leg is the only open space at the bottom.
  • Flat tops, flat feet. No rounding anywhere on the solid mark. Corners stay sharp.

10 · Rules

Keep it clean.

  • Clear space = one stem width (16 units) on every side. Nothing enters that box.
  • Minimum size: mark 24px / 8mm. Lockup 120px / 30mm. Below that, use the mark alone.
  • One colour only. Coyote, Bone, Ink or Mesquite. Never two colours inside the mark.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, outline the solid version, add shadows, add gradients, or put it on a photo without a solid panel behind it.
  • Don't set VALLE in any other typeface. One font family across roofing and interiors.
  • Don't use the AZ badge as the primary logo. It is a supporting piece.

11 · Hand-finish spec · For Tom

What to draw.

  • Redraw the mark by hand on the 100 grid using the construction on page 02. Keep stroke 16, bowl r22, arm 2:1, leg 1:2. Then export SVG, PDF, PNG (transparent) in Coyote, Bone, Ink and Mesquite.
  • Outline + monoline versions from the same centreline. Monoline stroke 5 units, round caps and joins.
  • Arizona outline: trace a real state outline, 2.2-unit stroke, round joins. Mine on page 03 is a placeholder shape.
  • Wordmark: set VALLE in the chosen font, then convert to outlines and tighten the LL and LE pairs by hand so the gaps match the V-A gap.
  • Lockups: stacked (mark above wordmark, gap = 1 stem), horizontal (mark left, gap = 1 stem, mark height = cap height of VALLE), badge.
  • Explore 2 or 3 of your own takes on the V to R idea too. Bring them to the review next to these.
