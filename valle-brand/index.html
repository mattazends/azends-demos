01 · The idea

The V becomes the R. One stroke, shared.

V for Valle. R for Roofing. The right arm of the V is the stem of the R, so both letters are built from one shape. Valle also means valley, which is exactly where Tucson sits. A letter, a valley and a roofline at the same time.

09 · Rules

Keep it clean.

  • Clear space = one stem width on every side. Nothing enters that box.
  • Minimum size: mark 24px / 8mm. Lockup 120px / 30mm. Below that, mark alone.
  • One colour only inside the mark. Never two colours, never a gradient.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, add shadows, or put it on a photo without a solid panel behind it.
  • Don't set the name in any other typeface. One font family across roofing and interiors.
