    How a project comes together

    From a survey to the day you move in.

    01 · SURVEY

    We start with what's already there.

    Measured surveys, drone photography and home scanning, so design starts from accurate drawings, not guesswork.

    02 · DESIGN WORKSHOP

    Ideas, shaped with you in the studio.

    Two-hour design workshops at our countryside studio, sketching and modelling options together in real time.

    03 · VISUALISE & PLANNING

    See it before it's built.

    3D visuals and drawings you can actually understand, then a planning application prepared and managed for you.

    04 · TECHNICAL & BUILD

    Drawn to be built.

    Building control, structural calculations and technical design, with the option to build with our partners.

    05 · LIVE IN IT

    A home that fits how you live.

    More light, better flow and lasting value, whether it's an extension or a once-in-a-lifetime country house.

    The design workshop

    Two hours that shape your whole project.

    Our design workshops are how every ZJS project really begins. You come to the countryside studio near Odiham and we design with you, around the table, in real time.

    Book a workshop ↓Prepare in the 3D designer
    How it works

    Not a meeting. A design session.

    BEFORE

    Send us what you have

    Your address or plot, a few photos and anything you've saved for inspiration. Your 3D designer brief works too.

    HOUR ONE

    Explore the options

    We look at the site and how you want to live, then sketch layouts, massing and character options together.

    HOUR TWO

    Test it in 3D

    The strongest ideas go into a quick model, so you can see light, scale and views before anything is drawn properly.

    AFTER

    Leave with a direction

    A clear design direction, the likely planning route and the next steps for budget and programme.
